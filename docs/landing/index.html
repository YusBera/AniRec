GETTING THERE

Three steps, and only one of them needs your account.

The order matters. You should see the thing working before it asks you for anything.

01

Unzip and run

One folder, one executable. Python and every library it needs are already inside. Windows 10 or 11.

02

Look around first

A sample library opens on first launch: a real feed, real breakdowns, no account. Connect MyAnimeList when you want the picks to be about you.

03

Argue with it

Like it, pass on it, save it for later. The taste model updates on every decision and the rest of the feed reshapes to match.

WHERE YOUR DATA GOES

Nothing leaves this machine.

AniRec reads your viewing history. That is a reasonable thing to be careful about, so here is the whole story.

On your disk

Ratings, tokens, generated results, and cached covers all live in your local AniRec application-data folder. Clear any of it from Settings.

One server, with permission

AniRec talks to the official MyAnimeList API and nothing else. You approve the connection in your own browser, and your password never reaches the app.

No telemetry

No analytics and no account on our side, because there is no our side. Logs are written locally and redacted before anything is stored.
