Procedure
01

Unpack and run

One directory, one executable. The interpreter and every library are inside it. Windows 10 or 11.

02

Run on sample data

A bundled library opens on first launch. A real feed and real breakdowns, with no account attached.

03

Attach your history

Connect MyAnimeList when you want the model fitted to you. Every decision you make refits it.
